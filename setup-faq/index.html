Frequently Asked Questions

Common fit and pricing questions, answered

Use this section to answer fit, payment, rollout, and review questions before launch.

Is HuddleWay a fit for my type of program?

HuddleWay is built for youth sports programs, including clubs, camps, leagues, and training businesses that want one branded app for registration, payments, schedules, and updates.

What does my team have to do before launch?

Keep it simple: create the account, add your logo and program details, set the registration and payment flow, then review what families will see before you share it.

What should families expect?

Families should expect one branded app where they can review the program, see schedules and updates, understand the next step, and pay the program directly when needed.

How do payments work?

Customers pay your program directly. Joining is free, HuddleWay adds up to a $1 platform fee per transaction, standard card fees still apply, and this is not a 30% app-store revenue-share model.

Can I use HuddleWay across multiple teams or divisions?

Yes. HuddleWay works well for programs that need a more consistent structure across teams, divisions, events, and family information. Plan the exact setup around how your organization already runs.

What should I review before launch?

Review the branding, dates, fees, registration step, payment expectations, schedules, and update path so the experience feels clear before you share it.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use HuddleWay?

No. HuddleWay is meant to feel straightforward for everyday program teams. The goal is a clear setup, a branded family path, and less manual upkeep once the program is live.
