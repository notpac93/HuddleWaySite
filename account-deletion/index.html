Account Deletion

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Use this page to request deletion of a HuddleWay Hub or HuddleWay Admin account without needing to reinstall or open the app.

Request Deletion From The Web

Send an account deletion request to support@huddleway.com. Include the email address used for the account, whether you used HuddleWay Hub or HuddleWay Admin, and the tenant or program name if you know it.

HuddleWay support may ask for additional information to verify account ownership before fulfilling the request.

Request Deletion In The App

HuddleWay Hub

Open your account screen and use the account deletion request option. That request is submitted to HuddleWay for account deletion processing.

HuddleWay Admin

Open App Settings and use the legal/support account deletion request option when it is available for your admin account.

What May Be Deleted

Approved deletion requests may remove or de-identify account profile information, contact information, device registrations, and other account-linked personal data where deletion is technically and legally available.

What May Be Retained

Some records may need to be retained for payment, accounting, fraud, safety, dispute, security, audit, backup, or legal reasons. HuddleWay may also retain records needed by a tenant operator to manage rosters, registrations, events, messages, or compliance responsibilities.

A deletion request is not the same as canceling a payment, resolving a team registration issue, or deleting tenant-owned operational records. HuddleWay support will route those issues as needed.

Need Help?

For questions about privacy choices, deletion status, or account access, contact support@huddleway.com.
